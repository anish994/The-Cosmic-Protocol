During Combat:

1. Load AI Strategy for player tier
   ↓
2. Detect enemy strategy (burst/control/sustain)
   ↓
3. Filter available combos by available skills
   ↓
4. Score combos:
   - Base: synergy × 100
   - Counter bonus: +50
   - Urgency bonus: +40 if health < 30%
   - Complexity bonus: +20 if diverse keywords
   ↓
5. Execute highest-scoring combo
   ↓
6. Suggest next combo from follow-ups
   ↓
7. Repeat
