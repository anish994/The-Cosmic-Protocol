## ⚖️ BALANCE SYSTEMS

### Power Scaling
```
Tier 0 (Beginner): 20-40 damage, low cost, short cooldown
Tier 1 (Competent): 50-80 damage, medium cost, medium cooldown
Tier 2 (Skilled): 90-130 damage, high cost, long cooldown
Tier 3 (Master): 140-200 damage, very high cost, very long cooldown
Tier 4 (Legendary): 250+ damage, extreme cost, once per chapter

Fusion Skills: +30% power, +50% cost, unique mechanics
```

### Resource Management
```
Bandwidth: 100 max (primary resource, regenerates 10/turn)
KP: 10 max (strategic resource, regenerates 1/turn or via meditation)
HP/Ojas: 100% max (health, regenerates slowly or via healing)
```

### Cooldown System
```
INSTANT: No cooldown, can spam (usually weak or utility)
SHORT: 2-3 story beats (1-2 combat rounds)
MEDIUM: 5-7 story beats (3-4 combat rounds)
LONG: 10+ story beats (full encounter)
ONCE_PER_CHAPTER: Ultimate skills, story-defining moments
ONCE_PER_PLAYTHROUGH: World-altering skills, ending triggers
```

### Anti-Exploit Measures
```
Same skill 5+ times → Effectiveness -30%
Boss sees skill 3+ times → Develops counter
Overuse corruption skills → Forced corruption arc
Spam healing → Enemies focus healer
Stealth spam → Guards increase patrols
```
