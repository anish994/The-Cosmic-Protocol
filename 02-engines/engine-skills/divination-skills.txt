# Divination Engine — Complete Skills List (v1.0)
## Total Skills: 100 (Foresight, Fate Manipulation, Knowledge, Temporal Mechanics)

---

### **CORE DIVINATION PATHS — 5 Specializations**

**Jyotish (Astrology)** — Celestial patterns and planetary influence
**Nadi (Fate Reading)** — Destiny threads and karmic manipulation  
**Samudrik (Intuition)** — Present awareness and hidden knowledge
**Kala (Time)** — Temporal manipulation and future sight
**Akasha (Records)** — Universal knowledge and memory access

---

### **CORE RESOURCES & MECHANICS**

**Drishti (Vision Points):** Primary resource for Divination abilities (Max 100)
**Darshan (Insight Stacks):** Accumulated knowledge that enhances predictions (0-20 stacks)
**Sutra (Fate Threads):** Connections to destiny that enable manipulation (3 max active)
**Kshana (Time Fragments):** Allow rewinding, pausing, or accelerating effects (5 max stored)

**Core Status Effects:**
- [Foreseen] — Next action revealed, enabling perfect counter
- [Destiny Bound] — Locked into predetermined outcome
- [Temporal Echo] — Action repeats next turn automatically
- [Knowledge Drain] — Enemy abilities revealed and weakened
- [Prophecy] — Future event guaranteed to occur
