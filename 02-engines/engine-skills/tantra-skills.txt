# Tantra Engine — Complete Skills List (v3.1 Expanded)
## Total Skills: 100 (40 Original + 60 Build-Defining)

---

### **Maran (Annihilation) — 4 New Skills**

1.  **Glyph: Resonant Detonation** (Catalyst, Gnosis 40)
    *   **Core Function:** Consume all *types* of Resonance on a target. For each unique Resonance type consumed (e.g., [Decay], [Stasis], [Discord]), deal 15 AoE damage around the target.
    *   **Evo A (Chain Reaction):** The AoE radius increases by 1 for each unique Resonance type.
    *   **Evo B (Focused Blast):** Damage is dealt to the primary target only but is increased by 50%.
    *   *Design Note: Encourages cross-karma skill weaving for a massive damage payoff.*

2.  **Glyph: Cull the Weak** (Execute, Gnosis 35)
    *   **Core Function:** Deal 20 damage to a target. If the target is below 30% Ojas, the damage is doubled and applies 2 stacks of [Decay].
    *   **Evo A (Ruthless Precision):** The execute threshold is increased to 40% Ojas.
    *   **Evo B (Spreading Plague):** On a successful execute (kills the target), applies 2 [Decay] to adjacent enemies.

3.  **Glyph: Withering Curse** (DoT, Gnosis 25)
    *   **Core Function:** Apply a debuff for 3 turns: "Target cannot be healed." At the end of the duration, the target takes damage equal to the healing they would have received. Applies 2 [Decay].
    *   **Evo A (Caustic Wound):** Healing prevented is increased to 125% of the damage dealt.
    *   **Evo B (Lingering Malediction):** The debuff duration is increased to 4 turns.

4.  **Mantra: Jvala (The Flame)** (Chant, Gnosis 15)
    *   **Core Function:** Chant to load the "Jvala" Bija into the Mantra Matrix.
    *   **Bija Passive:** Your skills have a +5% critical hit chance.
    *   **Stuti (Jvala + Aakrosh):** Your next damage glyph has +25% critical hit damage.
    *   *Design Note: Adds a critical strike subsystem to the Maran path.*

### **Vashikaran (Subjugation) — 3 New Skills**

5.  **Glyph: Glimpse of Betrayal** (Control, Gnosis 45)
    *   **Core Function:** For 1 turn, a target enemy Yantra's passive aura affects its owner and their allies instead. Applies 3 [Subservience].
    *   **Evo A (Sustained Treachery):** The effect lasts for 2 turns.
    *   **Evo B (Infectious Betrayal):** The effect also applies to another random enemy Yantra.

6.  **Glyph: Forced Allegiance** (Catalyst, Gnosis 50)
    *   **Core Function:** Consume 5 [Subservience] stacks from an enemy Architect. The next buff they cast on themselves is also applied to you.
    *   **Evo A (Perfect Mirror):** You also gain 10 Ojas when the buff is copied.
    *   **Evo B (Stolen Power):** The enemy does not receive the buff; only you do.

7.  **Yantra: The Overseer** (Yantra, Gnosis 40)
    *   **Core Function:** Deploys a Yantra with a passive aura: "Enemy Yantras in the same row cost +1 Prana to activate their abilities."
    *   **Resonance Link:** If linked with another Vashikaran Yantra, the aura affects the entire board.
    *   **Evo A (Oppressive Gaze):** The Prana cost increase is now +2.
    *   **Evo B (Resource Drain):** The Yantra also drains 1 Prana from the enemy Architect at the start of your turn.

### **Vidveshan (Discord) — 3 New Skills**

11. **Glyph: Shared Suffering** (Debuff, Gnosis 30)
    *   **Core Function:** Link two enemy Yantras for 3 turns. Whenever one takes damage, the other takes 50% of that damage. Applies 2 [Discord] to both.
    *   **Evo A (Amplified Pain):** The shared damage is increased to 75%.
    *   **Evo B (Chain of Agony):** You can now link up to three Yantras.

12. **Glyph: Corrupted Boon** (Catalyst, Gnosis 45)
    *   **Core Function:** Consume 4 [Discord] stacks from an Architect. The next healing or shielding buff they receive is converted into a [Decay] DoT instead.
    *   **Evo A (Inverted Grace):** The DoT's damage is equal to 150% of the healing/shielding that was prevented.
    *   **Evo B (Prolonged Corruption):** The effect now corrupts the next two boons instead of one.

13. **Yantra: The Chaos Spire** (Yantra, Gnosis 35)
    *   **Core Function:** At the start of your turn, swaps the current Ojas values of two random enemy Yantras.
    *   **Resonance Link:** If linked with another Vidveshan Yantra, you can choose which two Yantras to swap.
    *   **Evo A (Unstable Matrix):** The swap also applies 1 stack of [Discord] to both Yantras.

### **Uchatan (Banishment) — 5 New Skills**

14. **Glyph: Resonance Siphon** (Resource Denial, Gnosis 40)
    *   **Core Function:** Remove up to 5 Resonance stacks of a single type from a target and gain 1 Prana for each stack removed. Applies 1 [Void].
    *   **Evo A (Manaforge):** Also gain 1 Prana for activating the glyph.
    *   **Evo B (Resonance Shatter):** Instead of gaining Prana, deal 5 damage to the target for each stack removed.
    *   *Design Note: A powerful tool for countering other Tantra players.*

15. **Glyph: Un-naming Rite** (Banish, Gnosis 60)
    *   **Core Function:** Target an enemy Invocation glyph. If it has been cast this duel, [Banish] it for 2 turns. If it has not been cast, increase its Sanctity/Anarchy cost by 10. Applies 4 [Void].
    *   **Evo A (Memory Wipe):** Increases the Banished duration to 3 turns.
    *   **Evo B (Cost Inflation):** The cost increase is now a permanent +15.

16. **Glyph: Void Trap** (Trap, Gnosis 35)
    *   **Core Function:** Place a latent trap on an enemy. The next time they gain a resource (Prana, Insight, etc.), you steal 50% of it. Applies 2 [Void].
    *   **Evo A (Hungering Void):** You steal 75% of the resources instead.
    *   **Evo B (Resource Burn):** The enemy gains no resources, and you gain nothing. The resources are simply destroyed.

17. **Yantra: The Null Field Generator** (Yantra, Gnosis 50)
    *   **Core Function:** Deploys a Yantra with a passive aura: "Buffs cannot be applied to any unit (friend or foe) in this Yantra's row."
    *   **Resonance Link:** If linked with another Uchatan Yantra, the aura also prevents debuffs.
    *   **Evo A (Expanded Null Field):** The aura affects the two adjacent rows as well.
    *   **Evo B (Targeted Erasure):** The aura is disabled. Gains an activatable ability: "Pay 3 Prana to [Banish] all buffs on a target unit."

18. **Samputa: Shunya → Shunya → Shunya** (Ultimate)
    *   **Core Function:** A Samputa triggered by loading three "Shunya" Bija into the Mantra Matrix.
    *   **Effect (Absolute Nothingness):** For 2 turns, no player can gain new buffs, debuffs, or resources. All existing timed effects are paused.
    *   *Design Note: The ultimate stall and reset button.*

19. **Glyph: Dissonant Barrier** (Defensive, Gnosis 30)
    * **Core Function:** Gain a shield that absorbs 15 damage. If the shield is broken by an attack, the attacker is [Silenced] for 1 turn. Applies 1 [Discord].
    * **Evo A (Feedback Pulse):** When broken, also deals 10 damage to the attacker.
    * **Evo B (Resonant Shield):** The shield absorbs 25 damage but the [Silence] does not apply.

20. **Glyph: Stasis Web** (AoE Control, Gnosis 40)
    * **Core Function:** Apply 2 stacks of [Stasis] to all enemies in a target column.
    * **Evo A (Widened Web):** Affects two adjacent columns.
    * **Evo B (Sticky Web):** Also applies a debuff that reduces movement speed by 50% for 2 turns.
