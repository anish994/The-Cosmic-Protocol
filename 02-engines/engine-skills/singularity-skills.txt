# Singularity Engine — Complete Skills List (v1.0)
## Total Skills: 100 (Transcendence, Reality Warping, Godhood, Rule Breaking)

---

### **CORE SINGULARITY CONCEPT**

The Singularity Engine represents **transcendence beyond normal game mechanics** — breaking limits, rewriting rules, achieving godhood, and manipulating the fundamental fabric of reality itself.

---

### **CORE SINGULARITY PATHS — 5 Specializations**

**Brahman (Unity)** — Merge with universal consciousness, erase boundaries
**Moksha (Liberation)** — Transcend limitations, break free from constraints
**Shakti (Power)** — Ultimate raw power, exponential scaling
**Maya (Illusion)** — Reality manipulation, perception control
**Shunya (Void)** — Annihilation of concepts, erasure of existence

---

### **CORE RESOURCES & MECHANICS**

**Ananda (Bliss Points):** Transcendence energy (Max 100, regenerates slowly)
**Siddhi (Mastery Levels):** Permanent power levels (0-10, never decrease)
**Nirvana (Awakening Stacks):** Each stack grants +10% ALL effects (caps at 10 = +100%)
**Avatara (God Forms):** Transformation states that rewrite rules (3 max unlockable)

**Core Status Effects:**
- [Transcendent] — Immune to negative effects, +50% all positive effects
- [Reality Warped] — Game rules don't apply to affected entity
- [Godhood] — Can rewrite one game rule per turn
- [Singularity] — All opposing forces collapse into this entity
- [Erasure] — Targeted concept/entity ceases to exist
