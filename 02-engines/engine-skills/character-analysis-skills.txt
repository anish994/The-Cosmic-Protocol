**Design Philosophy Notes:**

These "Big Brain" skills reward:
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identifying opponent habits and playstyles
- **Predictive Modeling**: Forecasting future game states with accuracy
- **Risk Management**: High-reward plays that punish poor execution
- **Multi-Step Planning**: Thinking 3-5 turns ahead
- **Meta-Game Understanding**: Knowledge of engine interactions and optimal counters
- **Adaptive Strategy**: Adjusting plans based on new information
- **Information Asymmetry**: Converting knowledge into tactical advantage

Skill Floor: Low (can use basic functions with reduced effectiveness)
Skill Ceiling: **EXTREME** (mastery requires chess-level strategic thinking)
