## 💎 RESOURCE SYSTEM

### Primary Resource: **Sanctity** (0-100)
- Generation: +5/turn, +10 per heal/buff, +15 per [Sanctify]
- Purpose: Currency for all invocation glyphs

### Secondary Resource: **Grace** (0-30)
- Effect: +1% ALL effects per Grace (universal bonus)
- Generation: Through healing, blessing, sacrifice

### Miracle Tokens
- Generation: 1 per [Sanctify] effect
- Spending: 1-5 per miracle glyph

### Counterplay:
- Jack-of-all-trades (not strongest in any single role)

- Grace accumulation slow (10-20 turns)
- Can be rushed before scaling

### Caps & Limits:
- Sanctity: 80 soft → 100 hard → 150/200 ultimate
- Grace: 20 soft → 30 hard (removable with skills)
- Miracle Tokens: Generated 1 at a time (prevents spam)
- Conversion: 1:1 ratio (fair, not exploitable)
