## 2. RESOURCE SYSTEM & MECHANICS

### Primary Resources

**Jyoti (Celestial Energy): 0-100**
- Primary resource for all Jyotish actions
- Regenerates: 10/turn base, +5 per Tier 3 planet
- Enhanced by: Jupiter (×2), Venus (+50%), Sun (+30%)
- Consumed by: House upgrades, Yoga formations, Transit triggers

**Graha Shakti (Planetary Power): 0-10 per planet**
- Represents each planet's current influence level
- Gain: +1 per turn naturally, +3 during planet's Dasha
- Boosted by: Exaltation (double gain), Yogas (+2 instant)
- Lost through: Debilitation (-5), Combustion (-3), Enemy aspects (-1/turn)

**Bhava Points (House Energy): 0-100 per house**
- Used to upgrade houses to Level 10
- Gain: 5 points/turn in houses with planets
- Special: Planets in houses grant domain bonuses
- Strategic: Concentrate on 3-4 houses for mastery

**Darshan (Cosmic Insight): 0-999**
- Knowledge currency for advanced Yogas
- Gain: +10/turn base, +50 per completed Yoga
- Special uses: Unlock Divisional Charts (100 each), Trigger Transits (200)
- Jupiter/Ketu synergy: +300% Darshan gain

**Kshana (Time Fragments): 0-50**
- Manipulate turn order and action timing
- Gain: +1 per Dasha transition, +5 during Retrograde
- Spend: Delay enemy action (10), Take extra turn (30), Reset Dasha (50)
- Mercury synergy: Halve all Kshana costs

**Siddhi (Divine Accomplishment): 0-10**
- Ultimate power currency, very rare
- Gain: Complete Yoga (1), Win with specific strategy (2), Apocalypse survival (5)
- Spend: Trigger guaranteed Yoga (3), Force planet to Tier 3 (5), Rewrite game rule (10)
- Once per duel: Convert 100 Darshan → 1 Siddhi

### Secondary Mechanics

**Yoga Multiplier (1.0-10.0×)**
- Stacks from active Yogas
- Each Yoga adds +0.5-3.0× depending on rarity
- Caps removed when Jupiter at Tier 3
- Applies to ALL Jyotish effects

**Dasha Phase Bonus**
- Current Mahadasha planet gets +100% effects
- Antardasha (sub-period) adds +50% to secondary planet
- Pratyantardasha (micro-period) fine-tunes timing (+25%)

**Astronomical Precision**
- Exact degree positions matter (0-360°)
- Tight aspects (within 1°) trigger critical effects
- Retrograde stations = power spikes
- Eclipse points = permanent power anchors

- Permanent until Dasha changes

**[Vargottama]** 💎

**[Planetary War]** ⚔️

- Winner takes all the loser's Shakti

- Loser debilitated for rest of Dasha

## 4. AI ORCHESTRATION SYSTEM

### Strategic Director AI

**Core Objectives:**
1. Maximize Yoga formations based on matchup
2. Time Dasha transitions for power spikes
3. Protect vulnerable planets from enemy aspects
4. Build toward Apocalypse-tier Yogas
5. Adapt to opponent's engine choice

### Resource Allocation Matrix

**Early Game (Turns 1-5):**
- Priority: Raise 2 planets to Shakti 5
- Spend Jyoti on: 1st House (identity), 9th House (fortune)
- Hold: Kshana for mid-game timing
- Build: Darshan foundation (aim for 100+)

**Mid Game (Turns 6-12):**
- Priority: Form first major Yoga
- Spend: Bhava Points to reach House Level 7 in 2-3 houses
- Use: Kshana to sync Dasha transitions with power plays
- Accumulate: Siddhi from Yoga completions

**Late Game (Turns 13-20):**
- Priority: Achieve Tier 3 on 2+ planets
- Complete: Pancha Mahapurusha Yoga or Raj Yoga
- Deploy: Accumulated Siddhi for reality-breaking plays
- Setup: Apocalypse conditions (all resources at max)

**End Game (Turn 21+):**
- Trigger: Vimala Yoga (12th House mastery)
- Reality rewrite: Use Siddhi to guarantee victory conditions
- Infinite scaling: Remove caps through Jupiter
- Transcendence: Moksha protocol if survival impossible

### Matchup Adaptation

**vs Invocation (Divine Beings):**
- Counter: Saturn (judgment), Rahu (eclipse gods)
- Strategy: Time Saturn Mahadasha during enemy manifestations
- Key Yoga: Sade Sati on enemy (Saturn affliction, -90% effects)
- Win Con: Outlast and out-scale divine summons

**vs Singularity (Reality Warping):**
- Counter: Ketu (anti-matter), Jupiter (expansion beats contraction)
- Strategy: Reality anchor through 4th House mastery
- Key Yoga: Amala Yoga (rewrite their rewrites)
- Win Con: Establish cosmic law that overrides local reality

**vs Divination (Timeline Manipulation):**
- Counter: Rahu (chaos breaks prediction), Mercury (faster than foresight)
- Strategy: Mercury Mahadasha for action speed
- Key Yoga: Budha-Aditya (intelligence + authority)
- Win Con: Act before they can predict

**vs Tantra (Energy Manipulation):**
- Counter: Shukra (harmony absorbs energy), Mars (overwhelming force)
- Strategy: Chandra-Mangala Yoga for power + stability
- Key Yoga: Gaja Kesari (wisdom + emotion = transcendence)
- Win Con: Cosmic scale dwarfs local energy

**vs Mirror Jyotish:**
- Counter: Eclipse mechanics (Rahu/Ketu disruption)
- Strategy: Force asymmetric Dasha timings
- Key Yoga: Graha Malika (all planets chained)
- Win Con: Perfect timing beats similar power levels

## 34. ASPECT SYSTEM

- Stronger planet dominates
- Can trigger special Yogas

- Direct confrontation between planets
- Enemy planets = conflict (both weakened)
- Friendly planets = balance (both strengthened)
- Tension creates power (use opposition for energy)

**Trine (120° separation):**
- Harmonious flow of energy
- Always beneficial
- Automatic Yoga formation
- Trines between 3 planets = Grand Trine (reality-bending power)

**Square (90° separation):**
- Friction and challenge

- Benefics excel (Jupiter/Venus maximized)
