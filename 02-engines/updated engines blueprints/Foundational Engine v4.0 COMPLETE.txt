## 💎 RESOURCE SYSTEM — UNIFIED SCHEMA

### Primary Resource: **Bandwidth**

**Concept:** Simultaneous action capacity - enables multi-target effects and extended durations  
**Range:** 0-100 typical (soft cap at 80, hard cap varies by build)  
**Generation:** Gained through structures, support actions, resource economy skills  
**Purpose:** Unlock multi-target [Support], extend [Structure] duration, amplify effects

**Legacy Mapping:**
```
Skill List v3.1    →    Schema v4.0
────────────────────────────────────
Bandwidth          →    Bandwidth (0-100)
Gnosis             →    KP + Bandwidth (cost split)
Stability          →    Structure Uptime % (0-100%)
```

**Key Resource Interactions:**
- **Bandwidth:** Spend to [Support] +1 additional target per 10 spent
- **Stability:** Higher structure uptime % unlocks milestone bonuses
- **[Refresh]:** Reduce cooldowns (capped at 2 per turn baseline)
- **[Reduce]:** Lower costs on ally glyphs (temporary or permanent)

### Secondary Resource: **Stability** (Structure Uptime %)

**Concept:** Percentage of board covered by active [Structures]  
**Range:** 0-100% (3 structures = 60% typical, 5 structures = 100%)  
**Calculation:** (Active Structures / Max Structures) × 100  
**Bonuses:** Milestone rewards at 100%, 80%, 60%, 40%

**Stability Thresholds (Milestone Bonuses)**
- **100% Stability:** All glyphs cost -1 KP, permanent +10 Shield to all allies
- **80%+ Stability:** [Support] affects +1 additional target automatically
- **60%+ Stability:** [Refresh] can target 2 cooldowns (up from 1)
- **40%+ Stability:** Bandwidth generation +50%

**Bandwidth Economy**
- **Starting Bandwidth:** 30 (standard)
- **Max Bandwidth:** 100 baseline (expandable with specific skills)
- **Generation:** +10 per [Structure] active, +5 per [Support] cast
- **Spending:** 10 per additional target, 15 per duration extension
