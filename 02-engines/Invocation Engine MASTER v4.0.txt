2) ENGINE MECHANICS v4.0
Resources
- Sanctity (0–999), Anarchy (0–999), Dharma (-100…+100), Presence (0–10), Apocalypse (0–100)

Core Actions
- [Chant X]: add X stacks (tempo). Breakpoint: open Rite Window.
- [Consecrate]: fortify defenses or sanctify battlefield zones.
- [Invoke <EntityID>]: summon/possess/manifest based on Presence & resources.
- [Bind Pact]: convert upkeep cost types (e.g., Sanctity→Dharma).

Windows & Gates
- Rite Window: opened by 3 chants (modded by Jyotish/Mercury/Moon). Lowers costs, grants safety.
- Oath Chain: consecutive turns with successful rites build Presence faster (+2/turn).
- Sacrificial Tiering: pay Ojas/resources to jump a tier once per duel.

Upkeep
- Manifest/Locus cost per turn; replaced by Presence lock at Tier ≥2.

3) AI ORCHESTRATOR (RITUAL DIRECTOR)
Goals
- Choose which patron(s) to court based on matchup and board state.
- Manage chants to sync with Dashas/Transits and enemy cooldown valleys.
- Allocate resources to reach key tiers at decisive turns (T2/T3 spikes).

Heuristics
- If opponent has lethal next ≤2 turns → prioritize Theurgy shields (Michael/Raphael)
- If opponent is resource-snowball → Paimon/Set drain lines
- If DoT/attrition → Raphael/Zadkiel cleanse loops, Durga invulnerability
- If stall → Odin/Thor pressure into Ragnarok; Kali/Shiva enders
