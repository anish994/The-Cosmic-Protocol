## KEY TAKEAWAY

Modifiers don't just add numbers. They:
- **Transform skill behavior** (magic vs physical, AoE size, burn mechanics)
- **Enable team synergies** (Leo radiates power, Revati heals allies)
- **Create playstyle shifts** (House 12 ultimate-focused, House 1 core/defensive)
- **Scale exponentially** (House × Planet × Lagna × Nakshatra)
- **Reward smart placement** (right skill in right house = massive effects)

**The card becomes a LIVING SYSTEM that changes based on Lagna/Nakshatra rotation.**
