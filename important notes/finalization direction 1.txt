Skill System
Base Skills: 1,037+ skills, each with unique effects and some with passives.
Fusion: Fusing skills creates new, evolved skills with transformed effects, not just simple combinations.
Passives: Skills can have passives that amplify keyword/element synergies, but the real magic is in fusion and dynamic combos.
Card System
Affinity & Keyword: At creation, each card can optionally get a random keyword and element (affinity). These are the card’s “core identity.”
Evolution: Cards can gain new keywords/elements by spending resources (seeds, etc.)—not by fusing cards, but by evolving their own properties.
Synergy: Multiple elements/keywords on a card create new effects for the card and its skills, based on synergy rules (not fusion).
Gameplay Loop
No Card Fusion: Each card is a standalone powerhouse, but can evolve by adding new keywords/elements.
Jyotish System: The 12-house chart and planetary/nakshatra modifiers add another layer of dynamic buffs and synergies.
Building Blocks: Skills, elements, keywords, and Jyotish modifiers are all modular—combine them in any way for infinite combos.
